Privacy Policy

El Patio Tennis · Last updated November 2026

This policy explains what the El Patio Tennis website collects and how it's used. The site is used to book community tennis sessions and to help coaches manage their schedules.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your name, email address and, if you give it, your mobile number. We also store your session bookings, waitlist places, cancellations and attendance.

Coaches can record the names, times, locations, prices and notes for their own private and semi-private lessons. Those clients don't need an account. Players can't see these lessons.

How we use it

We use this information to run bookings and waitlists, show coaches their rosters, and manage the coaching schedule. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Google Calendar for coaches

Coaches can choose to connect a Google Calendar. If they do, the site uses Google's permission to view and edit calendar events only to create, update and remove events for that coach's own community sessions and lessons. It also reads the coach's Google email address to show which account is connected.

The site doesn't read other events on the calendar. A long-lived Google access token is stored securely so updates can keep running. It can't be read from the website.

Disconnecting from My Schedule removes the token and takes upcoming events from this site off the calendar. Past events stay. Coaches can also revoke access at any time in their Google Account.

The site's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

Coaches and admins can see player contact details and bookings so they can run sessions. Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and calendar events are stored by Google for coaches who connect a calendar.

Your choices

You can update your details on your profile page. To ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted, email leocanbakiri@gmail.com.
